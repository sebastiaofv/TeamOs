Project save lifecycle: one explicit state

Today a project's save status lives in two separate fields (status and watchStatus), two revision counters and an expiry timestamp, and three places in the app work out what they mean together. The question: can one named state (plus the revision counters and a short-lived notice) describe every case the app handles today, so the toolbar badge, autosave, the close warning and cross-project moves all read from the same place?
