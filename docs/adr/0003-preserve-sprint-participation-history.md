# Preserve Sprint participation independently of current allocation

A Story may span several Sprints, but Tasks completed in an earlier Sprint must not reappear as newly planned work when the Story moves. Preserve actual Sprint participation independently of current allocation instead of reconstructing every Task's past from its parent's current Sprint, so historical consultation remains meaningful without duplicating completed work. This does not introduce autonomous Sprint closure; its future frozen results must use the recorded participation rather than fabricate historical membership.
